Add text
Hollow Works on flat-sided shapes: text, lines, boxes, cylinders, hex prisms. Floor 0 leaves the bottom open. The top is always open.
Simplify (fewer triangles) Select a shape to see its triangle count. Removes the triangles that matter least. Curved surfaces stay smooth and sharp edges stay sharp. Big imported meshes get much faster to edit and slice. Undo brings the original back.
Round or chamfer edges Works on flat-sided shapes: text, lines, boxes, cylinders, SVG shapes.
Array Copies go round a vertical axis at the centre point. Move the shape away from the centre first.
Split by plane Cuts each selected solid in two along a world plane. Position starts at the selection centre.
Push / pull a face Tap a flat face.
Chamfer or round one edge Tap an outside straight edge.
Measure Tap a corner, edge or face. It snaps to corners first.
Shell (hollow out) Works on any closed solid. Open top or bottom needs a flat face there.
